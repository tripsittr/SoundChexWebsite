# 010 — Full platform coverage + cast/car on the roadmap (W-22)

*2026-09-17.*

## Done

- **Home page** "Runs where you do" row now lists every target, not just the six
  desktop/mobile ones: added Apple TV, Android TV, Fire TV, Roku, Smart TVs, and
  CarPlay & Auto. The caption links to the full roadmap.
- **Roadmap** gained the must-have in-app integrations (Planned):
  - Apple app: **CarPlay**, **AirPlay & Chromecast** (sender).
  - Android app: **Android Auto**, **Chromecast & AirPlay** (sender).
  - Server: **DLNA output** — advertise the library to DLNA devices (old TVs,
    consoles, receivers) that can play with no app.

## Why

The home page previously undersold the ambition (six platforms; TVs/Roku only
appeared on /roadmap). Cast and in-car are built into their respective native
apps, so they live under those platforms on the roadmap rather than as a
separate product. Pairs with app-side issues (iOS/Android cast + car; server
DLNA).
